*******************************************************************

** Core Truck Table (Minimum Real-World Fields) **

Here’s what a realistic trucks table usually includes:

** Identification **

truck_id (UUID, primary key)

unit_number (internal company truck number — VERY important)

vin (UNIQUE, required)

plate_number

plate_state

** Specifications **

make (Freightliner, Peterbilt, etc.)

model

year

color

fuel_type (diesel, etc.)

** Operational Fields **

current_odometer

status (active, maintenance, out_of_service)

in_service_date

ownership_type (company-owned, leased, owner-op)

** Administrative **

insurance_policy_number

registration_expiration

inspection_due_date

created_at

updated_at

*******************************************************************

Also consider creating a truck_odometer_logs table to track lastest readings.
